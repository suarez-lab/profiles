## Background

- **AEM development internship** (June 2024–March 2026) — Java, JavaScript and
  React development and REST integrations in an enterprise content platform,
  using Maven, Git and CI/CD.
- **MSc thesis** — spectral and matrix analysis of multiplication in discrete
  Sobolev spaces (9.5/10). The manuscript studies a Gelfand-type index through
  finite-codimension restrictions, bounded point evaluations, Hessenberg matrices
  and singular values. Its concrete circle-measure results and numerical scope
  are explained in the [thesis summary](theses/README.md).
- **BSc thesis** — zero localisation for Sobolev orthogonal polynomials (9.4/10).
  My computational work uses Maple to construct polynomial families, compute and
  visualise their zeros, and compare them with operator norms and singular values.
