# Aníbal Suárez Hernández

**English** · [Español](anibal.es.md)

**Systems architect · Electronic engineer · Madrid**

I design and operate production systems, and I have also carried the number at the
bottom of the page. Electronic engineer by training; thirty years of running
engineering and operations — team lead, department head, COO of two operating
telecom companies, services delivery director, business owner. That combination is
the point: I am used to being the person who has to say what a system will cost to
run before anyone has written a line of it.

Most of my recent work is AI-assisted business platforms on Google Cloud —
conversational systems, operational back-offices, data pipelines — for clients in
real estate, financial services and consumer/social platforms across Spain and LATAM.

Before that, twenty years in carrier and utility infrastructure: SCADA and control
centres, metropolitan fibre, SDH transport, metro ethernet, microwave and satellite
backbone, WiMAX. I have run P&L as COO of two operating companies, a US$100MM CAPEX
programme at a national carrier, and a EUR 22MM/year services delivery organisation
of 32 people — ISO 9001 certified under my lead. The largest team I have managed
directly is 65.

## What I actually do

- **Architecture under constraint.** Not the elegant version; the one that fits the
  budget, the legacy system nobody may touch, and a team of two.
- **Cloud cost engineering.** LLM spend in particular. Model selection, prompt and
  token discipline, guardrails that fail closed. A chatbot that is correct and
  unaffordable is not shipped.
- **Operating what I build.** Observability, alerting, incident response, and writing
  the lesson down afterwards.
- **Delivery.** Proposal, scope, spec, build, deploy, handover.

## Background

- **Own business** (2004–present) — real estate office under a national franchise,
  then independent consulting and delivery. Corporate planning and budgeting,
  recruitment and coaching, regulatory and fiscal compliance, digital marketing.
  Running a small company end to end is where the cost discipline above comes from.
- **Services delivery director**, global network equipment vendor (2011–2013) —
  EUR 22MM/year of services delivery for a tier-1 mobile operator, 32 people across
  care, consultancy, network planning, managed services and implementation projects
  (core, packet core, radio, civil works). Quality lead for the ISO 9001
  certification of the unit, obtained in 2011. Accountable for service, project and
  financial KPIs.
- **Access, interconnection and wireless manager**, national incumbent carrier
  (2006–2010) — 65 people, US$100MM CAPEX and US$1.5MM OPEX. Microwave and fibre
  backbone, fibre and radio last miles, satellite, WiFi, WiMAX; GIS deployment;
  frequency and site administration; led the government backbone interconnection
  programme.
- **COO of two operating companies** inside an international utility group
  (2001–2006) — a metro ethernet carrier (27 people, US$3MM income, 34 carrier and
  ISP customers, close to 200 active circuits) and a trunked-radio operator
  (22 people, US$1.5MM income, 160 commercial customers across 5 cities). Ran the
  ISO 9001 certification of the first one. Earlier, as VP of planning, development
  and maintenance, I took the same network from nothing to 100 services installed in
  18 months.
- **Fibre and telecom infrastructure**, electricity utility and mobile operator
  (1990–2000) — 200 km of metropolitan fibre deployed by blow-air across 52
  distribution substations; metropolitan fibre networks designed and built in 8
  cities; the first aerial fibre system on 230 KV transmission lines in the country;
  SCADA for a distribution network, including 50 RTU expanded and 20 replaced.

**Education.** Electronic engineer, Universidad Simón Bolívar. Postgraduate studies
